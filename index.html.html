Our Story

Crafting Timeless
Celebrations

Founded by Ahmed Din with a singular vision — to create celebrations so extraordinary they become the standard against which all others are measured. Al-Faqir Palace has been Lahore's most trusted name in luxury event execution for over two decades.

We don't simply decorate venues. We architect emotion. Every chandelier, every floral arrangement, every draped fabric is chosen with obsessive care to ensure your guests experience something they will never forget.

From intimate mehndi ceremonies to grand multi-thousand-guest weddings, we bring the same relentless pursuit of perfection to every event we touch.

Signature Services

Every service crafted with royal attention to detail and delivered with flawless precision

01

Wedding Marquee

Grand structures adorned with crystal chandeliers, cascading florals, and bespoke drapery that transform open spaces into palatial venues worthy of royalty.

02

Mehndi & Barat

Vibrant traditional ceremonies brought to life through rich colour palettes, folk-inspired staging, and décor that honours cultural heritage with modern elegance.

03

Lighting & Ambiance

Custom programmable lighting rigs, fairy light canopies, uplighting arrays, and atmospheric projection mapping that sculpt the perfect mood at every moment.

04

Stage Design

Bespoke stages featuring hand-crafted floral walls, dramatic velvet backdrops, and ornamental arches that frame your most important moments beautifully.

05

VIP Seating & Lounges

Plush royal sofas, gilded chiavari chairs, carved dastarkhaan settings, and exclusive VIP lounges furnished to the highest standards of luxury hospitality.

06

Corporate & Galas

Sophisticated, brand-aligned event environments for product launches, award galas, and corporate dinners — executed with boardroom precision and gala glamour.
