Our Story

Crafting Timeless
Celebrations

Founded by Ahmed Din with a singular vision — to create celebrations so extraordinary they become the standard all others are measured against. Ikhlas Marquee at Al-Faqir Palace has been the most trusted name in luxury event execution for over two decades.

We don't simply decorate venues. We architect emotion. Every chandelier, every floral arrangement, every draped fabric is chosen with obsessive care to ensure your guests experience something they will never forget.

From intimate mehndi ceremonies to grand multi-thousand-guest weddings, we bring the same relentless pursuit of perfection to every single event we touch.
